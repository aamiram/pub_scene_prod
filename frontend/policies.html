About Pub Scene

Pub Scene is Nagpur’s premier nightlife discovery and digital entry pass reservation platform. Our mission is to elevate the urban nightlife culture by offering instant visibility into who is performing, crowd dynamics, and the best venues in town before you step out.

What we do:

  • Club & Lounge Discovery: Curating top verified nightlife venues across Nagpur (Dharampeth, Civil Lines, Sadar, Wardha Road, Hingna).
  • Venue Table Reservations & Cover Charge Passes: Offering friction-free, instantaneous digital dining & lounge entry reservations and cover charge vouchers (Couple Passes and Stag Entry) with secure scannable QR verification.
  • Venue Partnerships: Empowering nightclub owners and event curators with real-time guestlist management, verified attendee tracking, and capacity control.

All pass reservations on Pub Scene are protected with state-of-the-art encryption and delivered directly to your device.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and transactional security are paramount to us. Our data handling policies are outlined below:

  • Information We Collect: When you reserve passes, we collect your Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, Instagram handle (optional), Age, and transaction identifiers. This information is required strictly to issue valid digital passes and verify guest entry at the door.
  • Zero Payment Card Data Collection: Pub Scene does not capture, store, or process credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords. Digital entry passes are issued directly to guests without collecting financial account information.
  • Data Sharing Policy: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Customer details are shared only with the specific partner club or event organizer where the reservation was placed, strictly for door check-in validation.
  • Security & Encryption: All data transmissions between your browser and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Cookies & Session State: We use minimal essential cookies to maintain security session tokens and preserve browsing state. No third-party tracking or advertising cookies are utilized.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Due to capacity constraints and partner venue reservation commitments, we enforce a transparent refund policy:

  • Venue-Side Cancellations: If an event or club night is cancelled or rescheduled by the organizer or venue, attendees are notified immediately via SMS/WhatsApp with updated pass details or re-entry privileges.
  • Support & Queries: For any booking or reservation queries, please email your Pass Code (#PUB-XXXXXX) to support@pubscene.in.

Shipping & Service Delivery Policy

As Pub Scene provides electronic dining reservations, lounge access vouchers, and venue cover passes, our delivery terms are as follows:

  • Delivery Method: All bookings are fulfilled through Digital Entry Passes. No physical merchandise or tickets are shipped via postal or courier mail.
  • Delivery Timeline: Pass issuance is Instantaneous (within 60 seconds) upon pass claim. A scannable digital QR pass and unique booking confirmation code are rendered directly on your screen upon confirmation.
  • Backup Delivery: In addition to instant on-screen rendering, booking confirmation details are dispatched via SMS / WhatsApp notification to the contact information provided.
  • Delivery Fees: Pass issuance is 100% electronic and free of charge.
  • Non-Receipt Resolution: If your digital pass does not appear immediately, please refresh your browser or contact support@pubscene.in with your name and event details for instant pass retrieval.

Pricing & Fee Policy

We maintain full transparency in our pricing structure:

  • Currency: All prices on Pub Scene are stated and processed exclusively in Indian National Rupees (INR / ₹).
  • Display of Rates: Pass pricing for each category (Couple Pass, Stag Cover, VIP Access, Early Bird) is clearly specified on every event card and prominently summarized before you proceed to payment.
  • Final Pricing: The amount displayed in the booking summary modal is the final payable amount inclusive of applicable platform charges and taxes. There are no surprise hidden checkout markups.
  • Free Events: Events marked with ₹0 price are completely free of charge and require zero payment processing.
