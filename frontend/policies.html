Cancellation & Refund Policy

As a SaaS and venue reservation manager, we adhere to a strict cancellation policy to protect our partner venues' capacity planning:

  • Standard Non-Refundable Policy: Under normal circumstances, all confirmed guestlist access and table reservation payments are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Venue Initiated Cancellations: Full refund if a venue booking or table reservation is cancelled by the partner venue or platform due to operational issues, double-booking, or unforeseen closures.
  • SLA Timeframe: Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment source within 5 to 7 working days.
  • How to Request: If you believe you are eligible for a refund due to a venue-side cancellation, please email your Reservation ID to support@pubscene.in within 24 hours of the reserved date.

Privacy Policy

Pub Scene is dedicated to securing your personal information. Our privacy practices are outlined below:

  • Data Collection: We collect your Name, Phone Number, Email, and Payment Transaction IDs to verify your identity at the venue and manage your table reservations.
  • Payment Details: We explicitly note that all card, banking, and sensitive payment details are handled securely by RBI-licensed payment gateways. These details are never captured, processed, or stored on our servers.
  • Data Sharing: We operate under a strict non-sharing policy. We do not sell or lease your personal data to third-party advertisers. Your reservation name and contact info are only shared with the specific partner venue you booked for entry validation purposes.
  • Cookie Usage & Retention: We use essential cookies to maintain user session state and improve platform performance. Your data is retained only as long as your account remains active or as required for legal accounting compliance.
